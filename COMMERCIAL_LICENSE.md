# Institutional and Commercial Licensing

**Status: policy and inquiry draft — not a license grant or an offer of fixed terms.**  
Last reviewed: 23 October 2026. Public project attribution: **Breathe4me**.

The proposed [application license](LICENSE) is custom source-available and remains
subject to rights-holder approval and legal review. This document explains when
separate authorization is intended. It does not make that draft effective.

## Which uses need authorization?

| Use | Intended licensing route |
| --- | --- |
| A student managing their own coursework on their own behalf | Free personal-use license, once finalized |
| An individual studying independently for noncommercial learning | Free personal-use license, once finalized |
| Private modification or self-compilation for that permitted personal use | Included in the proposed personal-use permission |
| School-managed deployment, a managed classroom installation, or an institutional service | Separate written authorization |
| Enterprise use, commercial hosting, paid tutoring or another paid service using the application | Separate written authorization |
| Nonprofit institutional deployment | Apply for authorization; a free grant may be considered but is not automatic |
| Distribution of modified installers or portable builds, or bundling the application in another product | Not granted by default; obtain specific written permission |

Being a student, nonprofit or educational organization does not by itself permit
institutional deployment or a paid service. Donations, sponsorship, advertising
and other borderline uses require clarification; no automatic exemption or
blanket prohibition is established here. GitHub platform rights and independent
third-party licenses remain unaffected.

## How to request authorization

**A verified licensing contact has not yet been published.** The public maintainer
identity is [Breathe4me](https://github.com/Breathe4me). A GitHub profile is not a
private messaging channel or proof of legal identity. Once the official project
provides a verified contact, send a brief request covering:

- The requesting person or organization's name and role.
- Personal, institutional, nonprofit or commercial purpose.
- Expected number of users/devices and whether deployment is centrally managed.
- Whether hosting, modification, redistribution or paid services are involved.
- Requested duration, support needs and any proposed distribution channels.

Do not include Canvas/PushPlus tokens, AI keys, student records, identity documents
or confidential commercial information in a public issue. A general public
inquiry can ask for a verified private licensing contact without disclosing them.

## What a separate agreement must settle

A written authorization must identify the actual licensor and licensee, covered
versions, authorized use, deployment scale, permitted modifications and any
redistribution rights. Duration, fees (if any), updates, support, privacy/data
responsibilities, termination and applicable legal terms must be agreed expressly.

No price, minimum purchase, free nonprofit approval, support entitlement,
exclusive right or service-level commitment is promised by this document.
Do not begin a use requiring authorization until the agreement is in place.
Third-party API charges and third-party software licenses remain separate.

## Third-party rights and affiliation

Separate application authorization cannot remove rights or obligations under
third-party licenses. See [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).
It also does not grant access to course data, institutional systems, trademarks
or another person's account. The project does not claim official endorsement
by Canvas, Instructure, the University of Sydney or another institution.

## Before this policy becomes final

The legal rights holder, ownership provenance, verified contact, application
license and enforceability of the final agreement still require confirmation.
Preparing these documents does not approve a public release or authorize
distribution of an unsigned installer.
