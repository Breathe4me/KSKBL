Default mode is the real application with an empty profile, not synthetic QA.
Enter your own Canvas credentials only in the application. AI and monitoring
start disabled. No automatic submissions, test messages or paid AI calls occur.

Canvas student API permissions and disabled Quiz endpoints may limit coverage.
Local completion is not Canvas submission. Check official deadlines and records.
AI is optional BYOK and providers may charge. PushPlus is a third-party service;
request acceptance does not prove WeChat delivery. Keep security protection on.

Installer/upgrade/uninstall GUI and real delivery/scheduling require manual QA.
Updates keep the v1.2 user profile. Uninstall removes this profile's scheduled
tasks and keeps settings, databases and DPAPI credentials. v1.1 stays separate.
